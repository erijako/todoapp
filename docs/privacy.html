Who we are

Onestep ("the app") is made by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, write to [CONTACT EMAIL].

What the app stores, and where

Everything you put in the app stays on your iPhone, in the app's private storage:

  • Tasks, thoughts and steps you write, and whether they're done, pinned or parked in Later
  • Your settings, such as Personal or Work mode
  • A running focus timer, so the lock screen and Dynamic Island can show it

The app's widgets and Live Activities read this data on your device to show your next step. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. We have no servers that receive your data.

What you write can be personal. That's exactly why it never leaves your device through us.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules them on your device (for example "Time's up" when a focus timer ends). They are created locally and don't go through any server of ours.

Purchases

Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment details under Apple's Privacy Policy. The app only checks on your device whether Pro is active. We don't receive your name, email or payment information.

To cancel a subscription, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Information Apple may share with us

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated statistics (such as installs or crash reports). This can't identify you, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, the app's data may be included in that backup. Backups are handled by Apple or your computer, not by us.

Your rights

Because we don't collect or store your personal data, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, export or delete. You're always in control:

  • Delete a task by swiping on it in the app.
  • Delete everything by deleting the app from your iPhone.

If you live in the EU/EEA (including Norway) or the UK, you have rights under the GDPR, and you can complain to your data protection authority. In Norway, that's Datatilsynet. We'd be glad to help first, so please write to us.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles data (for example if we add optional iCloud sync or sharing with a partner), we'll update this page and the date at the top before that feature ships. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app.
