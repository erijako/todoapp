# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

## Users
Adults with ADHD brains (diagnosed or not) who lose track of tasks the moment they are out of sight.
They pick up their phone ~100 times a day, often mid-overwhelm, low on energy, or task-paralysed.
Job: know the one next thing, and do it, without facing the whole pile.

## Product Purpose
Onestep is an iPhone to-do app that puts exactly one next step on the lock screen, checkable without
unlocking. Capture is a 2-second brain dump; the app decides "next" (pinned first, then order).
Success: the user does the next step more often, and never feels judged for what is left.

## Positioning
One next step on the lock screen, not a list. Competitors show lists or schedules; Onestep shows a
single step where the user already looks.

## Operating Context
Glances at the lock screen; quick capture while busy; a full-screen focus mode with an optional timer
and a Live Activity / Dynamic Island countdown; a gentle new-day card that offers to park tasks in
Later. Personal / Work modes. Data stays on device.

## Capabilities and Constraints
SwiftUI, iOS 17+, iPhone only, WidgetKit lock screen widget with interactive check-off, Live Activity.
Tasks, loose thoughts (never become "next"), steps inside a task, Later bucket, Done today list.
No dates, no overdue. Pro (focus timer + Live Activity) via StoreKit 2. Sharing (gentle reminders
from a partner) in v1.1. Lock screen widgets render monochrome/tinted by the system.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Onestep (lowercase wordmark allowed). Voice: "kind friend" — short, warm, a little funny
("Not your problem right now."). Never red, never "overdue", never streaks or guilt.
No medical claims ("built for ADHD brains"). Visual identity is open for redesign (2026-11-25).

## Evidence on Hand
No testimonials, users or metrics yet. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles
1. One thing at a time.
2. Zero-friction capture.
3. No shame: unfinished tasks quietly stay.
4. Make time visible.
5. Reward progress with small wins, not pressure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Text 4.5:1, controls 3:1, 44pt targets, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, light and dark mode.
Low-stimulation by default (audience is easily overwhelmed).
